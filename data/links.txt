DATA SOURCES - Iowa Liquor Sales 2025 + US Census ACS county data
=============================================================

1) Iowa Liquor Sales (transaction-level, Jan-Dec 2025)
   Title:     "Iowa Liquor Sales, 2025", Iowa Data Hub dataset 1262
   Publisher: Alcohol Operations Bureau, Alcohol & Tax Operations Division (State of Iowa)
   Licence:   CC BY 4.0
   Download:  https://idh-be.iowa.gov/api/v1/datasets/1262/rows.csv   (zip: iowa_liquor_sales_2025_1262_rows.zip)
   Metadata:  https://idh-be.iowa.gov/api/v1/datasets/1262/
              (numRows 2,490,620; coverage 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31; last updated 2026-10-05)
   Portal:    https://data.iowa.gov/
   Agency:    https://abd.iowa.gov/
   Files (data/raw/iowa_liquor_sales/): iowa_liquor_sales_2025_1262_rows_part_0001.csv ... part_0005.csv
   NOTE: part_0005 (429,989 rows, 2025-08-27 to 2025-10-30) is an exact copy of rows already in parts
         0003-0004, an artefact of the download zip. Parts 0001-0004 hold exactly the 2,490,620 published rows.
